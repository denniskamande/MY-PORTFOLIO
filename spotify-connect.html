Spotify setup

Connect your account

Sign in to Spotify and approve read-only access to playback status and recently played tracks. Your Spotify password is entered only on Spotify.

After approval, copy the refresh token into Vercel’s private environment settings.

In Vercel, add an environment variable named SPOTIFY_REFRESH_TOKEN with this value, then redeploy. Keep this token private and close this page after copying it. Spotify may require you to reconnect after the token expires or changes.
